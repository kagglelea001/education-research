The revised RDD estimates, based on QS 2025 data across 54 countries and nine outcome dimensions, yield 45 estimates (nine outcomes × five bandwidths). Of these, 24 are statistically significant: 17 positive and 7 negative. Employer reputation improves by +9 to +12 points (p < 0.05), international students by +28 to +39 points (p < 0.001), and faculty–student ratio by +33 to +42 points (p < 0.001). Citations show a robust negative effect at wider bandwidths (−29 to −39 points, p < 0.001), while sustainability (−21 to −25 points) and international research network (−17 points) decline significantly. Employment outcomes are positive at h = 12 and h = 15 (+11 to +16 points). The McCrary density test indicates an 11.1% discontinuity at the cutoff, suggesting no systematic manipulation. All estimates are robust across bandwidths, with treatment effects concentrated in teaching-related and internationalization metrics.
